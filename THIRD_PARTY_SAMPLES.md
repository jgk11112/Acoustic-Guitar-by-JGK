The upstream instrument `.sfz` header explicitly states:

- `2017 Martin HD28 Vintage Series`

Acoustic Guitar by JGK downloads the 15 mapped WAV recordings from that exact
pinned commit while configuring the build, then embeds them into the VST3.
This means the installed plugin does not need an internet connection or an
external sample folder.

The project name and UI do not claim endorsement by Martin, Ed Sheeran, Lowden,
or any other artist/manufacturer. The samples are used as a legal CC0 sound
source and are tone-shaped toward a compact contemporary singer-songwriter
acoustic character.
